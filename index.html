About NG Drop Taxi

South India's Trusted Intercity Cab Service

Dedicated to providing transparent, hassle-free, one-way and round-trip taxi services across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka (Bangalore), Kerala (Cochin), and Andhra Pradesh.

Revolutionizing Outstation Travel with Zero Return Fees

At NG Drop Taxi, we noticed that intercity commuters were often forced to pay full return fares even when traveling one-way between cities like Chennai, Bangalore, Madurai, Coimbatore, and Cochin.

We introduced an honest per-kilometer billing model. You pay strictly for your traveled distance. Our fleet of clean Sedans, SUVs, Innovas, and Tempo Travellers is ready 24/7 with experienced highway drivers to get you to your destination safely.
